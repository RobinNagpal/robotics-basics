## 1. What is a world model, and what does solution 4 do with one?

A [world model](03_how-it-works.md#2-what-a-forward-model-is) predicts what a
push will do: the table after the push, and whether anything toppled. Solution 4
tries many pushes on five copies of the model. It drops any push that one copy
says might topple a glass. Then it makes the best push that is left, as
[planning by sampling](03_how-it-works.md#6-planning-by-sampling-the-cross-entropy-method)
shows.

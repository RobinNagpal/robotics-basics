## 1. What does solution 1 do, and why does such a simple rule score so well?

It pushes a crowded glass a short way from the neighbour that crowds it, then
looks again. That direction helps whatever the friction is. The safe parts,
such as the tipping check, are shared by every solution. On 50 tables it racked
107 of 251 glasses, finished 14 tables and toppled one glass. See [where it is strong and where it
breaks](06_how-it-compares.md#1-where-it-is-strong-and-where-it-breaks).

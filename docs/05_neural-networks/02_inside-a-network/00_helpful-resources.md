# Helpful resources

This page lists videos made by other people that explain neural networks. It is
for a reader who wants a second explanation beside this chapter, or who learns
better from watching than from reading. Nothing in the chapter depends on these
videos, so you can skip this page and start at [neural networks and
neurons](01_networks-and-neurons.md), which is where the chapter itself begins.

The videos come from two sources. 3Blue1Brown is a YouTube channel that explains
mathematics with animated drawings. MIT OpenCourseWare publishes recordings of
lectures given at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).

## 1. Four videos to watch first

These four cover the same ground as this chapter and the one after it, so they
are the ones shown here to be played on the page. Watch them in the order given.

The first video draws a network that reads a picture of a handwritten digit. It
explains what a neuron, a layer, a weight and a bias are, which are the same words
that [neural networks and neurons](01_networks-and-neurons.md) introduces.

[But what is a neural network? (3Blue1Brown, Deep learning chapter 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aircAruvnKk "embed")

The second and third videos are the first two lectures of an MIT course on deep
learning. The first lecture introduces neural networks and starts on how they are
trained.

[Introduction to Neural Networks and Deep Learning; Training Deep NNs (MIT OpenCourseWare, lecture 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kyQ0CRkYhy4&t=6s "embed")

The second lecture goes on with training, and then builds a network with Keras
and TensorFlow, which are code libraries for building neural networks. Its link
starts the recording 1 hour, 5 minutes and 59 seconds in.

[Training Deep NNs (cont.); Introduction to Keras/Tensorflow; Application to Tabular Data (MIT OpenCourseWare, lecture 2)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Lr8nVUDCpk&t=3959s "embed")

The fourth video explains gradient descent, which is the method that finds good
values for the weights. It belongs with the next chapter, which starts at [the
score of being wrong](../03_how-training-works/01_the-score-of-being-wrong.md).

[Gradient descent, how neural networks learn (3Blue1Brown, Deep learning chapter 2)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHZwWFHWa-w "embed")

These four explain what a network is and how it is trained. The next section
lists two shorter videos that go one step further.

## 2. Two more videos

These two follow on from the videos in [four videos to watch
first](#1-four-videos-to-watch-first), so they are listed as links rather than
shown on the page.

- [Backpropagation, intuitively (3Blue1Brown, Deep learning chapter
  3)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ilg3gGewQ5U) explains how a network works
  out which way to change each of its weights. It follows straight on from the
  gradient descent video.
- [Large language models explained briefly
  (3Blue1Brown)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LPZh9BOjkQs) explains in a few
  minutes how the models behind chatbots use neural networks.

The chapter itself starts on the next page, [neural networks and
neurons](01_networks-and-neurons.md), which says what a neural network is in one
sentence.
